About the Artist

A contemporary artist working primarily in oil and watercolor, drawing inspiration from the natural world and urban landscapes. Each piece is an exploration of light, color, and emotion — an invitation to pause and see the beauty in everyday moments.

My work seeks to bridge the gap between the observed and the felt. Through careful attention to color and composition, I aim to create paintings that resonate on a deeply personal level, offering viewers a moment of stillness in an ever-moving world.
